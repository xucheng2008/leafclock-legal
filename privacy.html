PlantCare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

PlantCare ("the app") is published by Cheng Xu. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short because the app collects nothing.

1. Data we collect

None. PlantCare has no account, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Plant names, photos, care schedules and growth-log entries you create.
  • App settings (language, theme, reminder times).

All of it is stored locally in the app's own container and in the shared App Group container used by the Home Screen widget. It never leaves your device, except when you choose "Export" and share the file yourself.

3. Photos and camera

The app asks for photo-library and camera access only when you add a photo to a plant or a growth entry. Photos you select are copied into the app's storage. We have no access to them.

4. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally with Apple's notification framework. No server is involved.

5. Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple provides the app with a purchase receipt; we do not receive your name, payment details or address. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. There is no server-side copy to delete.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions: xucheng20081@gmail.com
