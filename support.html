PlantCare — Support

PlantCare is a plant watering reminder with a seasonal schedule and a growth timeline. No account, no subscription.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings inside the app → "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Reminders are not arriving

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → PlantCare is allowed, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not silencing them. Open the app once after changing any schedule so notifications can be rescheduled.

The widget shows the wrong number

Open the app once to refresh the shared data, then pull down on the Home Screen or remove and re-add the widget.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Export. You get a zip containing plants.csv, care-plan.csv, plant-log.csv and your photos.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything is stored locally, so there is nothing left on any server.
